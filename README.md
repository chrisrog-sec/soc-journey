## Investigation Methodology

Investigation writeups follow a consistent structure:

1. **Alert Summary:** What triggered the investigation?
2. **Scope & Context:** Which users, hosts and processes are involved?
3. **Investigation Steps:** What evidence was examined and why?
4. **Evidence & Timeline:** What did the logs and telemetry reveal?
5. **MITRE ATT&CK Mapping:** Which techniques are supported by the evidence?
6. **Verdict:** True positive, false positive, benign activity or inconclusive.
7. **Recommended Actions:** Containment, remediation, escalation or further investigation, as appropriate.
8. **Lessons Learned:** What did the investigation demonstrate?

Conclusions are based on available evidence, with uncertainties and investigative limitations documented where relevant.
